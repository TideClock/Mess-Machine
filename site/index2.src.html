      The Mess Machine

      Loop

      Tap a pad to play it. Hold a pad (or right-click it) for its menu: keys under the pads, doctor it, swap the sound, mute, solo, resample or clear. Double tap an empty pad for every sound in the lab. On a keyboard, 1 to 4, Q to R, A to F and Z to V play the pads.

      Pick a loop letter up top, then tap a bar to paint it in (right-click goes back). A dot is a bar of silence. Switch to Play the song and hit play.

      Mud filterdrag me

      Hold an effect, let go and it snaps back. They work on the pads too. Bottle it saves the clean version. Space plays and pauses; Ctrl or Cmd Z undoes.
