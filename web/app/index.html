Mess Machine

The web build of the app. The sound is the Android app's own engine, so a mess plays the same here as on your phone.

  • New: the Synth page, the Roll, Padiano and Chords under any pad (Pack), FX vials, LFO and XY per pad, Drop, the Sample sheet, Learn, exports and skins.
  • Try: swipe down off a pad for its Padiano; draw a circle on a pad for a sphere.
  • Not here: mic and MIDI (claude.ai blocks them; Record to pad picks a recording instead).
