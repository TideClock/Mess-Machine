Delete your account

This page deletes your account for the Mess Machine app by . You do not need the app to do it.

What deleting does

This goes

  • Your online messes and their history. A mess you own is deleted for everyone in it.
  • Your online sounds. They also go out of other people's messes.
  • Your share links and invites.
  • Your place in other people's messes.
  • Any sound you sent to the Vault that is still waiting.
  • Your name, your email and your sign-in.

This stays

  • The messes and sounds on your phone. The app keeps working without an account.
  • Any sound the Vault already accepted, with your credit name.
  • The words of reports, so we can keep acting on them.

It happens right away and cannot be undone. Our backups keep deleted data for up to 7 days, then it is gone from those too.

Not set up yet

Online accounts are not switched on yet, so there is nothing to delete here. Nothing is wrong on your side.

    Account deleted

    Your account is deleted.

    The messes and sounds on your phone are still there, and the app keeps working without an account. You can close this page.

    Cannot sign in?

    Write to from the email your account is under and ask us to delete it. We answer within a few days.
