Terms

Terms version 2026-11

These are the rules for a Mess Machine account. The app is made by . You only need to agree if you make an account. The app works without one.

1. Who can have an account

You must be 13 or older. If you are not yet an adult where you live, a parent or guardian has to agree for you.

2. What you make is yours

You own your messes and your sounds. We only need your permission to store them and to send them to you and to the people you share with. That permission ends when you delete them, except for a sound the Vault accepted (section 8).

Only put sounds online that you made or have the right to use.

3. Sharing

When you invite someone into a mess, they can change it and they see your name. Anyone who has a share link can open it. You can remove people, block them and turn links off at any time. You answer for what you share.

4. What is not allowed

We have no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. In messes, sounds and names, none of this:

  • Sexual content, and anything sexual involving a minor in any form.
  • Hate toward people for who they are.
  • Harassment, threats or bullying.
  • Other people's music, recordings or voices without their permission.
  • Spam, scams or selling things.
  • Anything against the law.
  • Breaking in, getting around limits, or loading the service on purpose.

5. Reports and blocking

You can report a mess or a person in the app, and a shared mess on its link page. You can block a person: they leave your messes and cannot invite you. We look at every report and act on it within 24 hours. Acting means taking the content down and removing the person who posted it.

6. What gets an account removed

Doing anything in section 4. Posting other people's work again after it was taken down. Making a new account to get around a block or a ban. Sending false reports on purpose. We can remove an account without warning. Its messes come down and its share links stop working.

7. Limits

An account holds up to 200 online messes and 250 MB of sounds. A sound can be up to 100 seconds and 20 MB. A mess can have up to 8 people. These numbers may change.

8. The Vault grant

Vault grant version 2026-11

By sending this sound to the Mess Machine Vault you say that you made it, or have the right to share it, and you let Mess Machine include it in the app and on its website for anyone to play and use in their own music, free of charge, with credit to the name you give. If it is approved it stays in the Vault even if you later delete your account. A sound still waiting for review when you delete your account is withdrawn and deleted. You keep your own rights to the sound.

9. Leaving

You can delete your account at any time, in the app or at Delete your account. That page says what goes and what stays.

10. No promises

Mess Machine online is offered as it is. We work to keep it running and your things safe, but we cannot promise it will always be up or that nothing will ever be lost. Keep copies of what matters: the messes on your phone stay yours whatever happens here. As far as the law allows, we are not liable for losses from using the service. If we ever shut it down, we will say so in the app first.

11. Changes

When these terms change, the version changes and the app asks you to agree again before you save anything more online.

12. Law and contact
