# REVIEW B: sync, client and app side of /home/claude/mess-machine/docs/ACCOUNTS-SPEC.md (sections 3, 4, 5, 8b, 8c, 9, 10). Nothing was edited in the repo.

1. VERDICT: build after the must-fixes. Server-ordered ops with last writer wins is the right design. Five rules as written are wrong, and the phase order puts the first useful build behind the hardest code.

2. MUST FIX (ranked)
M1. Reopening from the cache sends old work again (4.2: `pending = Ops.diff(base, host.project())`).
- What breaks [Certain, demonstrated]: I modelled 4.2 literally on the real Project class and ran it. Run 1: one phone, nobody else, swap pads 1 and 2, close, reopen: the swap is "found again", sent a second time and undoes itself. Run 2: A is playing with one batch of B's held; A clears loop B (confirmed); B then records 4 hits into loop B; A's app is killed and opened again: A's phone sends the clear again and B's take is gone on every phone (4 hits before, 0 after). Run 2 still fails under the kindest reading of the spec.
- Why: the cached base is the mess at shownRev, but the screen also holds my confirmed batches still in `log`; the diff cannot tell those from new edits. The spec also never says how a batch is marked "mine"; by user id, two phones of one account treat each other's edits as already on screen and never show them.
- Better way (paste): "The cache is two files. `<mess>.base.json` = {rev: shownRev, base}, written after a release. `<mess>.queue.json` = {sent: [{id, ops}], flight: {id, ops} or null, waiting: [ops]}, written before every `apply_ops` call and after every answer; `sent` is my confirmed batches above shownRev. On open: screen0 = base + sent + flight + waiting; pending = flight + waiting + Ops.diff(screen0, host.project()). A batch is mine only if its id is in `sent` or equals `flight.id`, never by user id. A flight batch that `ops_since` does not return is sent again under the same id. My confirmed batches at the head of `log` fold into base at once. Going to the background flushes and writes both files." Also make a swap safe to repeat: `pad.swap {a, b, ka, kb}` does nothing unless pads a and b still hold keys ka and kb.
M2. The next-loop hook (4.6, 5.2) does not deliver decision 2 as written. [Certain from the spec text and Engine.kt, except where marked]
- (a) A phone that only listens never gets the other person's edits: the held snapshot is armed only inside `rebuild()`, `rebuild()` runs only on a local change, `wrapSwaps` moves only when an armed snapshot was swapped, and `loopTop()` is called only when `wrapSwaps` moves. No SyncHost callback fires when a batch is held.
- (b) "`setSnapshot` leaves a held snapshot in place": press Stop with one armed, edit for two minutes, press Play: the first wrap swaps in the two-minute-old snapshot and the phone plays an old mess until the next touch.
- (c) 5.2 calls `localChanged` after the rebuild, so `preview()` is built without the edit just made; at the wrap that edit drops out of the audio until the next frame.
- (d) `render()` reads `snap` once per block (`val S = snap`, Engine.kt line 405) and hands it to every `fireStep`, so a swap made inside `fireStep` is not used until the next block. [Likely] If the held snapshot is a plain field, main can arm a newer one while audio clears the old one, and the newer one is lost.
- (e) `loopTop()` releases all of `log`, including batches that came in after the swapped snapshot was built; those land mid-loop.
- Better way (paste into 4.6): "`fun setSnapshotAtWrap(s: Snapshot?, token: Int)` goes through `cmds` like play and stop, so only the audio thread holds it; the stop and pause commands clear it. `Snapshot` gets `@JvmField var seq`, the host's count of local rebuilds. At a wrap (line 947, and the reset at line 866) the audio thread swaps only when `armed.seq == snap.seq`, then sets `@Volatile swapToken = token`; otherwise it waits one pass. `render` uses `var S` and reads `snap` again after each `fireStep`. Host: `arm()` = `session.preview()` (the session notes `previewRev`), build, `setSnapshotAtWrap(it, ++token)`. `arm()` runs at the end of `changed()` after `localChanged`, and from a new `SyncHost.onHeld()` the session calls when someone else's batch enters `log` while playing; at most once a frame. When `swapToken` changes, `session.loopTop(previewRev)` releases only up to that rev." With the seq rule the local player never hears a snapshot missing their own last edit.
- Recording into the pass being replaced: your own hits are never held, so they sound at once and stay in `pending`. If the other person cleared or replaced that loop, earlier passes of your take vanish on the downbeat and only the last pass stays. Add `rec` to presence and make `loop-ask` also say "Mia is recording in loop B".
M3. Ops from diffing two Projects, as specified, are too slow for drags and lose intent on structural edits.
- Cost [Certain, measured on this server's JVM]: for a 75 KB mess `Project.copy()` is a JSON round trip: 2.0 ms and 2.5 MB of garbage. `Ops.change` is two diffs that each start with `before.copy()`: 4.7 ms and 5.1 MB per change; with something held, `preview()` adds 2.3 ms and 3.2 MB. Today's whole per-edit cost (`Snapshot.build`) is 0.6 ms and 0.6 MB. `changed(false)` fires on every touch move of a fader. [Guessing] phones are 3 to 8 times slower, so 15 to 50 ms a move and about ten times today's garbage, in an app whose audio thread is a JVM thread on a two-burst buffer: [Likely] dropped frames and more underruns. Risk 10's "1 to 5 ms" counts one copy; a release makes three.
- Intent [Certain from 4.1 and the code]: "more than 64 field ops in a loop becomes one `loop.put`" turns a row tool on one busy 8 bar row into a replace of the whole loop, wiping other people's edits on every other pad in it. `loop.len` clears the new bars but `AppState.setLen` fills them with a copy, so doubling a busy loop goes out as `loop.len` plus `loop.put`. `DoctorScreen` keeps its own copy of the SoundEdit (`remember(key)`, line 46) and writes it back whole; the diff turns its stale fields into ops, so my volume drag undoes your pan. `AppState.tick` line 888 writes motion and calls `rebuild()` without `changed()`; a release before the next change throws that motion away.
- Better way (paste into 4.1): "`Ops.change(shadow, project)` makes no copies: it compares the arrays in place, writes each changed field's old value into `undo` in the same pass, then patches `shadow` in place. Structural edits are named, not guessed: `SyncSession.hint(op)` is called by the AppState actions that are structural (setLen: `loop.len {l, len, fill}`; copySlotTo: `loop.copy`; clearSlotAt: `loop.clear`; swapPads: `pad.swap`; putOnPad, clearPad, newSynth, scratchToPad: `pad.put`). `localChanged` applies the hints to `shadow` with the same `Ops.apply` every phone runs, then sends whatever still differs as field ops, so a missing hint costs intent, never correctness. More than 64 changes in one pad's row of one loop become `row.put {l, p, row}` (scope `R/<l>/<p>`); `loop.put` comes only from hints and undo. A release starts with `localChanged(false)`. `Ops.clone(p)` is a field-by-field copy (Pattern, SoundEdit, PadOpts and Patch already have `copy()`), used for base, shadow, preview and next." Make the in-place path the normal one for batches of field ops; keep the rebuild for structural ops. DoctorScreen re-reads its copy when `version` changes.
M4. In a solo mess, mutes, solos, song or loop mode, the picked loop and the scratch synth never reach the cloud. [Certain from 3.3 and 4.2] 3.3 says they "ride only in snapshots"; 4.2 builds the snapshot from `base` plus `log`, and `base` changes only by server ops. Phone 2 opens the mess unmuted and in loop mode. Better way: "When the mess has one member, the upload is `uploadDoc(carryLocal(host.project(), base + log))`, and a change to a 3.3 field makes a snapshot due."
M5. A batch the server refuses has no rule, so the queue never drains. [Certain the rule is missing; Guessing how often] 4.2 lists no answer to `too_big` or `bad_input`; one `loop.put` of a full 64 pad, 8 bar loop measured about 48 KB against the 64 KB cap. And on `forbidden` the spec drops `pending` and reverts the screen, losing the work. Better way: "Send the longest run of waiting ops that fits 400 ops and 48 KB; a single bigger op goes alone; the server cap rises to 256 KB. Before `pending` is ever dropped, the screen's mess is kept as a local copy."

3. SHOULD FIX
S1. FLAC is not needed for decision 7. [Certain, arithmetic] Workbench files are already 16 bit mono WAV (`Wav.writeMono16`): 5.3 MB a minute at 44.1 kHz, so 250 MB holds 45 to 49 minutes, more than the 38 the decision quotes. Upload the WAV as it is first (`fmt: "wav"` in SoundRef, path `<owner>/<sha>.<fmt>`, bucket allows both types); add FlacEnc later to halve storage. That takes FlacEnc, `Wav.pcm16`, `Sha256.kt` (use the platform digest) and ffmpeg in CI out of the first builds.
S2. Threads in 4.3 and 4.5. [Certain as written] The core runs on the main thread and `CloudFiles` is synchronous, so SoundSync would read, encode and hash a 100 second sound on the UI thread, and a synchronous file API cannot be built in a browser. One REST thread (5.2) also means a 50 MB upload holds up `apply_ops` for minutes. Better: replace `CloudFiles.read/write/sha256` with `fun prepareUpload(key: String, cb: Done<Prepared>)` (path, sha256, bytes, seconds, rate), done by the platform off the main thread; two queues in SupabaseApi, one for RPC and one for files; uploads use `setFixedLengthStreamingMode`.
S3. A sound that finishes downloading calls `rebuild()` at once, so a new take starts sounding mid-loop (decision 2): send it through `arm()` while playing. Today's toast "N pads use sounds this phone does not have" will fire on every open of an online mess: skip keys listed in `sounds`. Say whether `acct-data` covers downloads: a first-time invitee on mobile data would see silent "Arriving" pads with no way forward; the pad face should offer "Get 3 sounds now".
S4. Slot addressing after a swap. [Certain] B's ops for pad 1 that are in flight, pending or made offline land on the other sound once A swaps pads 1 and 2. Better: every pad op also carries `k`, the key the sender saw; apply to pad `p` if it holds `k`, else to the pad that now holds `k`, else to `p`. Two people putting different sounds on one slot is fine as is: the later one wins everywhere.
S5. Undo. Record `seenRev` (shownRev when the change was made) instead of the rev it was confirmed at, and skip when `touched > seenRev`; otherwise undo can put back a value older than a change the person had not yet seen. `edit.set` of `st` or `en` is not its own inverse (the `en <= st` reset moves the other field): send them as one op `edit.trim {p, st, en}`.
S6. `Ops.squash` needs `Ops.replaces(op)`, true only for `loop.clear`, `loop.put`, `loop.copy` (its `to`) and `pad.put`. `pad.swap` and `loop.len` have scopes but replace nothing; squashing a fader move that sits before a swap loses it. [Certain from 4.1's wording]
S7. A phone with `sawNewer` still sends `loop.put` built from a copy that lacks the newer ops, wiping them. Better: such a phone sends cells, never a put built from its own copy. iOS and web will lag Android, so this will be the normal case.
S8. `save_snapshot` drops `mess_sounds` rows not in the doc. [Likely] Undo of a pad clear, or a History restore, brings the key back but nobody can attach it again (`attach_sound` refuses a file that is not yours and no longer listed). Keep the rows for the life of the mess.
S9. Format v4. Bumping `v` makes every older build, and the v3 iOS and web apps, say "made by a newer version" for every mess, guests' too. If the bump stays (shared/README asks for it), add a reader rule: entries for keys not on a pad are dropped, because a v3 app carries the map untouched and it goes stale. `canon` must also define the junk cases: options and lanes left on an empty pad (`Project.assign` leaves them), all-empty lanes, and `lfoCut` and `lfoRes` (3.1 says they reset on load; `PadOpts.fromMap` keeps them).
S10. Tests (8b, 8c). The fuzz proves phones agree, not that they are right: both M1 runs end with every phone identical. Add: (1) kill, not close: reopen from the cache and the local file as last written; (2) a phone that changed nothing between close and open sends zero ops; (3) each phone edits its own pads and loops, and the end must equal a plain Project given the same edits; (4) an oracle that shares no code with `canon`: every phone bounces 4 bars and the bytes match; (5) EngineTest: an armed snapshot is heard from the exact wrap frame with blocks of 64, 480 and 8192 frames, in song mode, with a shorter loop arriving, on a phone nobody touches, and after Stop then Play; (6) two AppStates driven through AppState's own methods (setLen, scratchToPad, row tools, the Doctor open on one, XY motion recording); (7) SupabaseApi against a local HTTP and websocket stub, because no release build runs cloud code before 8e.
S11. `SEND_MS` 200 sits exactly on the 5 a second limit; two batches bunched by the network give `rate_limited` and a one second stall. Use 250 ms or allow 8.
S12. Section 9 says phases 4, 5 and 6 do not depend on each other. [Certain] Live editing needs a second member, and invites are in phase 4.
